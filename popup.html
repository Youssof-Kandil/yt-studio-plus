What you can do

  • 📝
    Upload templatesFill the whole upload form in one click: title, description, tags, playlists, audience, category and more.Left menu → Upload templates
  • 🔢
    Series modeSpots series in your titles and offers "Continue series → Ep 13" with the next number filled in.Side panel while uploading
  • 📂
    Video switcherYour other videos beside any video's page. Jump between them, or copy an older video's description and tags.“My videos” tab on the right · Alt+V
  • 📊
    Compare videosTwo videos side by side on the same timeline: views, watch time, CTR, retention and traffic sources.Left menu → Compare videos
  • 🔎
    Find & replaceChange a link or sponsor line across many descriptions or titles, or add and remove tags in bulk. Preview first, Undo after.Content → select videos → Find & replace…
  • 🔥
    Outlier scoreA badge like “2.4×” next to every view count, showing how a video does against your usual one.Content page
  • 👍
    Likes & dislikesLikes, dislikes and like ratio back on your video list and overview cards.Content page
  • 💬
    Saved repliesReusable comment replies that fill in the commenter's name. You still press Reply.Under any comment reply box
  • ↪️
    Redirect Shorts in bulkPoint many Shorts at one long video as their related video, all at once.Content → Shorts → select Shorts
